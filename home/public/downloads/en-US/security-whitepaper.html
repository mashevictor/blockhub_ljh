BlockHub Information Security Whitepaper

Enterprise assessment · data residency · encryption & access · deletion · BH-SEC-WP-2026.07 · 2026

Document overview

For enterprise IT and security teams — BlockHub practices for storage, transport encryption, access control, and deletion.

Cross-check with the Trust Center; for full DPA and compliance alignment, book a demo for the qualification pack.

Data storage & cross-border

Customer business data (app config, KB docs, forms, etc.) is stored by default in mainland China data centers.

Without written customer authorization, data is not used for model training or shared with third parties for marketing.

Cross-border access requires explicit enablement and supplemental terms; overseas admin login is off by default.

Transport & encryption

Admin and employee endpoints use HTTPS/TLS 1.2+ end-to-end; APIs support mTLS (hybrid/private optional).

Data at rest uses AES-256; keys in cloud KMS or customer HSM (private deploy optional).

Sensitive fields (phone, ID, etc.) support column-level masking and export controls.

Access control & audit

Role-based access (RBAC) with org/dept/store isolation.

Critical actions (login, permission changes, export, agent publish) are fully logged — default 180 days, extendable to 1 year.

CSV/JSON export supported; field reference in audit log sample.

Deletion & exit

Within 30 calendar days of contract end or written request, data is deleted with confirmation letter.

Backups cleared per rolling policy; sub-processor deletion confirmation available.

Review recommendations & next steps

Have security, architecture, procurement, and business owners review together and mark gaps against your questionnaire.

Gap items can become a customized response pack after a demo — avoid verbal promises in contracts.

Agree pilot acceptance in writing: permission matrix, audit export sample, confirm-before-write-back, and rollback conditions.

Commercial path: materials review → demo / PoC → quote & DPA → go-live and ops handover.

Terminology

“Default” means standard product configuration; hybrid/private may adjust per proposal.

“Supports” means capability or connector templates exist — not zero-effort go-live.

“Reference range” is for commercial discussion; the stamped quote and order govern.

“Verifiable” means logs, reports, or acceptance minutes can corroborate pilot outcomes.
